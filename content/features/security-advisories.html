Embargoes

Gated Advisories That Are Actually Gated

Some advisories are for everyone. Some are only for the customers running the affected product, under NDA, until the fix ships. Each advisory has one of three visibility states:

    {{< check-list-item title="Public" description="Readable by anyone once it leaves draft, indexed by search engines, and included in your CSAF feed." >}} {{< check-list-item title="Gated" description="Readable only by people with approved access who can already see at least one affected product. A customer of one product never learns about an embargoed issue in another." >}} {{< check-list-item title="Private" description="Never leaves your workspace, whatever its status." >}}
Discretion

Built Not to Leak

For an embargoed vulnerability, the fact that an advisory exists is itself sensitive. sbomify treats it that way.

    {{< check-list-item title="404, not 403" description="A reader who may not see a gated advisory gets "not found", not "forbidden", so its existence is not confirmed." >}} {{< check-list-item title="Products counted, not named" description="When an advisory spans several products, readers only see the names of the products they are entitled to." >}} {{< check-list-item title="Kept out of search" description="Gated pages are noindex, nofollow, and never appear in the public CSAF distribution." >}}
Machine-Readable

CSAF 2.0 Out of the Box

Enterprise customers don't read advisories one by one. Their vulnerability management tools pull them in automatically and match them against their inventory. sbomify publishes every public advisory as a CSAF 2.0 document, ready to be pulled in.

    {{< check-list-item title="CSAF provider metadata" description="Served at /.well-known/csaf/provider-metadata.json on your Trust Center domain, the entry point CSAF aggregators look for." >}} {{< check-list-item title="ROLIE feed" description="A TLP:WHITE feed listing every public advisory, so new ones are picked up without anyone sending an email." >}} {{< check-list-item title="security.txt with a CSAF pointer" description="Your RFC 9116 security.txt links to the CSAF provider automatically, laid out as BSI TR-03183-3 expects." >}} {{< check-list-item title="VEX vocabulary, mapped" description="Product status uses the CISA VEX-to-CSAF crosswalk, so not affected, under investigation and fixed mean the same thing in every format." >}}

Cyber Resilience Act

Vulnerability Handling the CRA Can Audit

The EU Cyber Resilience Act makes disclosure an obligation. Annex I Part II asks manufacturers to publish information about fixed vulnerabilities, run a coordinated disclosure policy and give researchers a contact. Article 14 adds reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities from 11 September 2026.
