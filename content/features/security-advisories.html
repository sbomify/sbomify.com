Embargoes

Gated advisories. That are actually gated.

Some advisories are for everyone. Some are only for the customers running the affected product, under NDA, until the fix ships. For those, even the fact that an advisory exists is sensitive.

{{< ph "lock-key" >}}

Gated per advisory

A reader needs approved access, with your NDA signed, and access to at least one affected product. A customer of one product never learns about an embargo in another.

{{< ph "prohibit" >}}

404, not 403

A reader who may not see a gated advisory gets “not found”, not “forbidden”, so its existence is never confirmed.

{{< ph "list-numbers" >}}

Products counted, not named

When an advisory spans several products, readers only see the names of the ones they are entitled to know about.

{{< ph "magnifying-glass-minus" >}}

Kept out of search

Gated pages are noindex and nofollow, and never appear in your public CSAF distribution. Private advisories never leave your workspace.

Machine-readable

CSAF 2.0 out of the box. For the tools that read it.

Enterprise customers don't read advisories one by one. Their vulnerability management tools pull them in and match them against their inventory.

  • {{< ph "file-code" >}}Provider metadata and a ROLIE feed, the entry points CSAF aggregators look for, so new advisories are picked up without anyone sending an email.
  • {{< ph "envelope-simple" >}}A security.txt that points to them, laid out as BSI TR-03183-3 expects.
  • {{< ph "shield-check" >}}VEX vocabulary, mapped with the CISA VEX-to-CSAF crosswalk, so not affected, under investigation and fixed mean the same in every format.

Cyber Resilience Act

Disclosure the CRA can audit. With the numbers to prove it.

The CRA makes disclosure an obligation. Annex I, Part II asks you to publish information about fixed vulnerabilities, run a coordinated disclosure policy and give researchers a contact.

  • {{< ph "megaphone" >}}Disclose fixed vulnerabilities with the description, affected products, severity and remediation the CRA lists.
  • {{< ph "timer" >}}Measure your patch SLA. Set a fix target per severity and the CRA KPIs follow, the numbers an auditor will ask for.
  • {{< ph "calendar-x" >}}Announce end of life through the same channel. sbomify drafts the notice and pins the final SBOM and VEX; a person publishes it.

Reporting actively exploited vulnerabilities under Article 14 is separate: that goes to ENISA, not your customers. How sbomify helps
