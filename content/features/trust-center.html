Access control

Public, gated or private. Per product.

Not every document belongs on the open web. Choose what visitors can see, component by component.

  • {{< ph "globe" >}}Public: listed and downloadable by anyone who visits.
  • {{< ph "lock-key" >}}Gated: listed, but visitors request access and accept your NDA first. You approve each request from your dashboard.
  • {{< ph "eye-slash" >}}Private: hidden from the Trust Center entirely.

Gated advisories go further: a reader who may not see one gets “not found”, so an embargo stays an embargo. How advisories are gated

Questions

Before you start

How is this different from a compliance trust center?{{< ph "plus" >}}

Compliance trust centers from GRC platforms hold SOC 2 reports and policies: they show the company is run securely. sbomify's is a product security trust center. It holds those documents too, and also answers what is in each release and whether a vulnerability affects it. Compare the two

Can we use our own domain?{{< ph "plus" >}}

Yes, on Business and Enterprise. Set a domain such as trust.yourcompany.com, add a CNAME record with your DNS provider and wait for it to validate. Setup guide

Can we share penetration test reports without making them public?{{< ph "plus" >}}

Yes. Mark them as gated: they stay listed, visitors request access and accept your Company NDA if you uploaded one, and you approve or deny each request from your dashboard.

Do we have to upload SBOMs by hand?{{< ph "plus" >}}

No. Send them from CI with the sbomify GitHub Action or your pipeline of choice, and the Trust Center reflects each new release as soon as it is uploaded.

Can we run sbomify on our own infrastructure?{{< ph "plus" >}}

Yes. sbomify is open source and runs with Docker Compose. Read the self-hosting guide, or use our cloud at app.sbomify.com.
