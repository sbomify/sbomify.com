Vulnerability handling

A new CVE just dropped. Now what?

Knowing a dependency has a vulnerability is only the beginning. Which of your supported releases contain it? Are you actually affected? Does it need a patch? What do you tell your customers?

sbomify links the finding to the products and releases that ship it, so your team goes from an alert to a recorded decision, a tracked fix and a published advisory.

Less time reconstructing your software history. More time fixing what matters.

{{< comment >}}AI section{{< /comment >}}
AI-enabled, not AI-powered

Your agents. Your data. Your decisions.

Your AI agents already know your codebase. Why send it to another vendor's model?

sbomify doesn't bundle an AI model, and none of its core security functions depend on one. Instead it gives your own agents structured security data and an API built for them, with a dedicated agent token that can read your workspace and propose work, but never approve it.

You choose which agents and model providers to use, so you decide where your data goes.

Use your agents to:

  • →Investigate a CVE in the context of your own code, using the triage and fix prompts sbomify generates
  • →Propose VEX statements that explain why a release is or isn't affected
  • →Draft security advisories and propose updates to published ones

A person signs off. VEX statements from an agent wait in a review queue, and its changes to published advisories are shown to your team as a diff. Nothing takes effect until a person approves it, and an agent can't approve its own work.

{{< comment >}}Audit trail{{< /comment >}}
Evidence

An audit trail you build by doing the work

The hard part of compliance isn't producing a document. It's showing what happened, when and why.

sbomify keeps SBOMs tied to the releases they describe and never alters them. Every triage decision is a new VEX version. Each finding records when it was first seen and when it was resolved, and advisories keep a timeline of every change. When customers, auditors or market surveillance authorities ask, you have the record.

CRA coverage

What sbomify covers in the CRA, and what it doesn't

No tool makes you CRA compliant on its own. Secure design, risk assessment and conformity assessment are still your work.

sbomify supports most of the vulnerability-handling requirements in Annex I Part II, tracks support periods, and drafts the documents you need: the EU declaration of conformity, vulnerability disclosure policy, security.txt and Article 14 notification templates. Our CRA page maps each obligation to what sbomify does.
