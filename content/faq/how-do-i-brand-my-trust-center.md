## Setting your branding

1. Open **Settings** and go to the **Branding** tab
2. Turn on **Use custom branding**
3. Under **Colors**, pick a **Brand color** and an **Accent color**, or type the hex values from your brand guide. The accent color is used for highlights on your public pages
4. Under **Logo and icon**, upload your **Logo**. It goes in the Trust Center header (1200 × 300 px PNG or SVG works best). The **Icon** is a square fallback for when there is no logo
5. Click **Save changes**

The **Preview** shows your saved branding. Your customers see changes only after you save, and then right away. Use **Open Trust Center** to check the public page.

To go back to the default sbomify look, turn off **Use custom branding**. Your colors and assets stay saved.

## What visitors see

Visitors see your logo in the Trust Center header and your accent color on the page highlights. The severity colors on security advisories deliberately do not follow your brand, so a critical finding never renders in a friendly color.
