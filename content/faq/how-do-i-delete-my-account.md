## Deleting your account

To delete your account in sbomify:

1. Navigate to **Settings** and open the **Account** tab
2. Scroll to the **Danger zone** and expand it
3. Click **Delete account**
4. In the confirmation modal, type `delete` and click **Delete my account**

## What happens next

Immediately:

- Your account is deactivated and you can no longer sign in
- Your personal access tokens are revoked, so any CI/CD pipelines using them stop working
- Your pending invitations are removed
- Workspaces where you are the only member are deleted, including all their products, SBOMs, and documents, and their subscriptions are canceled

After **14 days**, your account is deleted for good. If you change your mind, contact support within those 14 days and the deletion will be stopped.
