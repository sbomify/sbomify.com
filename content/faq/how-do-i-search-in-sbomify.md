## Using search

- **Open it:** press **Control K**, or **Command K** on a Mac, from any page. You can also click the search box at the top of the page.
- **Before you type,** it suggests common pages and example searches.
- **Pick a result** with the arrow keys and press **Enter**, or click it.
- **Other words work too.** Type `api key`, and it finds **API tokens** in Settings.
- **Your own data:** it finds your products and components by name, listed under **Products and components**.

Search also runs actions. Type `upload` to reach **Upload SBOM** (see [How do I upload an SBOM?](/faq/how-do-i-upload-an-sbom/)), or `connect a repository` to get back to the repository setup page from the [Get started wizard](/faq/how-do-i-use-the-sbomify-setup-wizard/).
