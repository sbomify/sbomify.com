The clock is running. Reporting applies today.

Article 14 reporting

24 hours to report. Start with the answer.

Once you know a vulnerability is actively exploited, the early warning is due within a day. Its first questions are inventory questions: which product, which versions, where. sbomify already knows.

Questions

Before you start

Will sbomify make us CRA compliant?{{< ph "plus" >}}

No tool can. Compliance covers how you design, test and assess your product as well as how you handle vulnerabilities after release. sbomify runs the second half and drafts much of the paperwork; the obligations guide shows exactly where it fits.

Does sbomify submit Article 14 reports for us?{{< ph "plus" >}}

No. sbomify drafts the early warning, the notification and the final report, and you submit them through the ENISA Single Reporting Platform. Register your EU Login accounts and representatives there before you need them.

We already run a vulnerability scanner. Why sbomify?{{< ph "plus" >}}

Scanners find vulnerabilities. sbomify uses Google OSV and Dependency-Track to do that, and adds what the CRA asks for around them: SBOMs pinned to releases, support periods, VEX decisions with their history, and advisories your customers can read.

Can we run sbomify on our own infrastructure?{{< ph "plus" >}}

Yes. sbomify is open source and runs with Docker Compose. Read the self-hosting guide, or use our cloud at app.sbomify.com.

Which plan do we need?{{< ph "plus" >}}

The free Community plan covers SBOM management, OSV scanning with weekly rescans and a public Trust Center. Business and Enterprise add the CRA Compliance Wizard, daily rescans, Dependency-Track, private documents and a Trust Center on your own domain. See pricing.
